What happens in Elche

IMED Elche Hospital is where I operate and where hospital tests are done; I also see patients there through Traumacare Medical Group. Your first assessment can be in Elche or in Torrevieja, whichever suits you.

  • Knee arthroscopy: anterior cruciate ligament, meniscus and cartilage
  • Ankle surgery: chronic instability and ligament repair
  • Minimally invasive percutaneous foot surgery (MIS)
  • Lemaire tenodesis in high-risk ACL reconstructions
  • Consultations, surgical planning and reviews

Frequently asked questions

Where will I have surgery if I live in Elche?

At IMED Elche Hospital, in Elche Parque Empresarial, the practice's reference hospital for surgery and hospital care.

How long is the hospital stay?

It depends on the procedure. Many arthroscopies are short-stay; the plan for your case is explained in consultation.

Can I also have my consultation in Elche?

Yes. Through Traumacare Medical Group I see patients at IMED Elche. Say so when booking.

Do you see patients in English?

Yes. Consultations are in English or Spanish.

Frequently asked questions

Before your first visit

Do you really speak English?

Yes — consultations, explanations and follow-up are fully in English. A large part of my practice are international residents of Torrevieja, Orihuela Costa and the wider Costa Blanca.

How do I book an appointment?

Call the Torrevieja clinic on +34 681 87 65 04 or book online 24/7 through my Doctoralia profile. If surgery is needed, it is scheduled at IMED Elche or IMED Alicante after your consultation.

Do you accept health insurance?

Consultations at Clínica Salar (Torrevieja) are private (self-pay). At IMED Elche and IMED Alicante I also see patients with Spanish private insurance through the Traumacare group. Tell me your situation when booking and I'll point you to the right route.

Do you offer online consultations?

Yes — for initial guidance and second opinions, bookable via Doctoralia. Bear in mind that a full assessment may require a physical examination and imaging in person.

Is an injury holding you back?

The earlier a knee or ankle problem is assessed, the more options there are to treat it without major surgery. First visit includes a full examination and, where useful, an in-clinic ultrasound scan.

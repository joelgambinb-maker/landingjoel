What happens in Alicante

At IMED Alicante I see patients and operate through Traumacare Medical Group. It is the base for patients from Alicante city and its surroundings, and for those who come through their health insurer.

  • Orthopaedic consultations: knee, ankle and foot
  • Knee arthroscopy: anterior cruciate ligament, meniscus and cartilage
  • Ankle surgery and percutaneous foot surgery
  • Joint-preservation treatments
  • Surgical planning and reviews

Frequently asked questions

Do you see insured patients in Alicante?

Yes, through Traumacare Medical Group at IMED Alicante. Mention your insurer when booking to confirm cover.

Can I have the consultation and the operation at the same hospital?

Yes. At IMED Alicante both the consultation and the surgery and hospital stay take place.

Where is IMED Alicante?

In the Vistahermosa Norte area of Alicante. The "Get directions" button opens the exact location in Google Maps.

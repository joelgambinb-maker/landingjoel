Orthopaedic surgeon in Torrevieja

Private sports-traumatology clinic at Clínica Salar in the centre of Torrevieja. Knee, ankle and foot, with surgery at the IMED hospitals in Elche and Alicante. Consultations in English and Spanish.

What happens at the Torrevieja clinic

Torrevieja is where I see patients: the injury is assessed, the treatment is decided and follow-up takes place here. Surgery is scheduled from this clinic at the IMED hospitals in Elche and Alicante.

  • Assessment of knee, ankle and foot injuries, with a full examination and in-clinic ultrasound when useful
  • Diagnosis and treatment plan: conservative whenever possible, surgery when indicated
  • Joint-preservation treatments: ultrasound-guided injections, hyaluronic acid and PRP
  • Second opinion on a diagnosis or a proposed operation
  • Post-operative reviews and follow-up

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. You can book online 24/7 through Doctoralia, call +34 681 87 65 04 or write on WhatsApp to the same number.

Do you operate in Torrevieja?

No. Surgery takes place at IMED Elche Hospital or IMED Alicante with Traumacare Medical Group. The consultation, planning and reviews are done in Torrevieja.

Do you see patients in English?

Yes. Consultations are in English or Spanish, and you can write on WhatsApp in English.

Do you accept health insurance?

At Clínica Salar consultations are private. Through Traumacare Medical Group, patients with Spanish private health insurance can also be seen at IMED Alicante; mention your insurer when booking.

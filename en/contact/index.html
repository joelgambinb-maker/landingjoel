What to bring to your first visit

  • Any previous medical reports related to your injury — in any language; Spanish or English are fine.
  • Imaging (X-rays, MRI, ultrasound) on CD, USB or as a digital link.
  • Comfortable clothing that allows the knee or ankle to be examined.

No imaging yet? No problem: the first visit includes a full examination and, where useful, an in-clinic ultrasound scan; anything else needed is arranged from there.

Online consultations

For initial guidance and second opinions I offer video consultations, bookable via Doctoralia. A complete assessment may still require an in-person examination.

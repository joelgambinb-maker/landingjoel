My story

I graduated in Medicine from the Catholic University of Valencia and completed my specialist training in Orthopaedic Surgery and Traumatology (the Spanish MIR residency) at Hospital Marina Baixa in Villajoyosa, Alicante. From early on, my focus was the athlete's musculoskeletal system: I completed a Master's degree in Sports Traumatology (UCAM) and an arthroscopic surgery fellowship at HLA Vistahermosa (Alicante), with further training placements at leading Spanish centres — Clínica CEMTRO (Madrid), Quirónsalud El Pilar and Clínica Tres Torres (Barcelona).

During my years in Valencia I served as head of medical services at Levante UD, a Spanish professional football club, leading the first team's medical department, and as a knee surgeon at IMSKE Hospital, where I was tutor of the Knee Surgery Fellowship, training other surgeons. I have also worked as a tournament doctor at the Davis Cup in Valencia, and in 2022 I received the 1st Bruce Reider Award — the national research prize of the Spanish Society of Sports Traumatology (SETRADE).

Today I'm back home in the south of Alicante province: I see patients privately at Clínica Salar in Torrevieja and operate at IMED Elche and IMED Alicante with the Traumacare group. I work the way I learned in professional football — precise diagnosis, decisions shared with the patient, and objective recovery criteria — applied to everyone, whether you're an athlete or simply want to walk without pain again.

What defines my practice

  • Real subspecialisation: knee and ankle, arthroscopy and sports trauma. I don't do a bit of everything — I do what I have trained in depth to do.
  • Preserve before replacing: repairing the meniscus whenever possible, treating cartilage early, and reserving major surgery for when it is genuinely indicated.
  • Objective criteria, no guesswork: return to sport and activity is decided with tests and data — the same way it's decided for a professional footballer.
  • Straight talk: you should leave the consultation understanding exactly what's wrong and what your options are, with their pros and cons. In plain English.
